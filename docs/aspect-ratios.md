## Shape by destination

| Destination | Suggested ratio |
|---|---|
| Instagram feed post | 4:5 or 1:1 |
| Stories, Reels cover, TikTok | 9:16 |
| Blog featured image / Open Graph | 16:9 or 2:1 |
| Full-width website banner | 3:1 or 21:9 |
| Pinterest pin | 2:3 |
| Tall sidebar / skyscraper ad | 1:3 or 9:21 |

Notes:
- Only GPT Image 2 / 2.5 offer 3:1, 1:3, 2:1, 1:2 and 9:21.
- Leave an empty area in wide images for headlines, and keep the subject away from the edges because phones crop wide images.
- Prompt boxes accept up to 4,000 characters.
