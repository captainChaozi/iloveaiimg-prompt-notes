# ILoveAIImg credit expiry and cancellation

Source: [Refund Policy, cancellation and credit expiry](https://iloveaiimg.com/refund-policy) · Plans: [pricing](https://iloveaiimg.com/pricing) · Home: [ILoveAIImg](https://iloveaiimg.com/)

## When credits expire

| Credit type | Expires |
|---|---|
| Monthly plan credits | End of the paid billing period |
| Annual plan credits | End of the 12-month paid period |
| New one-time pack credits | 180 days after purchase |
| Free daily allowance | Daily; unused credits do not roll over |

Previously purchased permanent credits keep their original validity. Expiry does not create a right to a cash refund.

## Cancelling is not refunding

- Cancelling an auto-renewing subscription stops future renewals. It does not refund the period you already paid for.
- A refund request does not cancel future renewal either. Do both separately if you need both.

## Practical habit

Plan big batches early in a billing period, and use pack credits within 180 days.
