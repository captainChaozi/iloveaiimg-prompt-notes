# ILoveAIImg: choose PNG vs JPG on export

Site: https://iloveaiimg.com/

Use a transparent **PNG** after [Background Remover](https://iloveaiimg.com/free-remove-background) when you need alpha. Prefer JPG for flat photo-like posts without transparency after finishing in the [AI image editor](https://iloveaiimg.com/ai-image-editor). Continue editing before you lock the download.
