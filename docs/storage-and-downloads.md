# Storage and downloads: keep what you make

How long images stay in your iLoveAIImg workspace, from the [pricing page](https://iloveaiimg.com/pricing) (checked 2026-11-02). App: [iloveaiimg.com](https://iloveaiimg.com/).

## By plan

| Plan | Storage | Credits |
|---|---|---|
| Free ($0) | Images saved for only **7 days**; basic storage space | 6 credits a day, up to 3 images a day; daily credits expire and do not roll over |
| Pro ($20/month) | **Permanent storage** for all your assets; expanded workspace storage | 1,000 credits each paid month |
| Max ($80/month) | **Permanent storage**; largest workspace storage | 5,000 credits each paid month |

Paid plan cards also list full downloads with no added watermark. Prices and limits may change; check the pricing page.

## Free plan habit: download as you go

1. When a result is good, download it straight away from **Recent images**.
2. Name the file with the date and the prompt idea, so you can find it later.
3. Keep your source photo and the prompt text alongside the result.
4. Do not rely on the workspace as an archive on the free plan; after 7 days the image is gone.

## Before deleting or replacing

If you continue editing an image, download the version you like first. Each edit produces a new result, and you may not get the same one again.
