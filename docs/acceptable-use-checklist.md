# Before you upload: an ILoveAIImg acceptable-use checklist

Policy: [ILoveAIImg Acceptable Use Policy](https://iloveaiimg.com/acceptable-use-policy) · Tools: [ILoveAIImg](https://iloveaiimg.com/)

The policy applies to every ILoveAIImg tool: prompts, uploads, reference images and generated results. The short version is that you should only process content you have the right to process. A quick pre-upload check:

## Whose face is this?

- Do you have the rights, authorization and consent to use this person's face or likeness?
- Face swaps, deceptive deepfakes, identity replacement and impersonation are not allowed.
- Sexualized edits of a real person, or content made from private images without permission, are not allowed.

## Could the result be mistaken for a real record?

Media presented as an authentic record of an event that did not occur, fabricated evidence, and political content that falsely depicts a real person or event are prohibited.

## Is the source yours to edit?

Unauthorized commercial use of protected characters, brands, artwork or distinctive styles is out of scope.

## Everyday edits are fine

Background removal for your own product photo, a new backdrop for your own picture or a poster from your own brief are the kinds of tasks the tools are built for. See the [AI image editor](https://iloveaiimg.com/ai-image-editor) and [background remover](https://iloveaiimg.com/free-remove-background).

This is a summary, not legal advice. The policy page is the authoritative text.
